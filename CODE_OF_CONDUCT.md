# Code of Conduct

Quizzer is built in public by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

If a situation needs private review, contact the maintainers through the repository’s private security-advisory channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, review threads, community spaces, and public representation of the project.
